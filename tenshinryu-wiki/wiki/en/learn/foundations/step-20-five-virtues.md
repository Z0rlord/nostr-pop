# Step 20 of 21 — The Five Constant Virtues

**Why this step:** The ethical spine of the warrior class, drawn from Confucian teaching and sewn — literally — into the hakama. Sekishūsai himself tied these virtues to the license to teach.

- [[sources/kuwami-foundations-principles-vol1/ch-23-the-five-constant-virtues-jin-gi-rei-chi-shin|Vol. 1, Ch. 23 — Jin, Gi, Rei, Chi, Shin]]
