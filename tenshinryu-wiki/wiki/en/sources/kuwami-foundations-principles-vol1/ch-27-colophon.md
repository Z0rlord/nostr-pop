Author Profile ;

Tenshinryu Hyoho 10th Shike

鍬海 政雲 一心
Kuwami Masakumo Isshin

Born in 1978 (Showa 53) in Hokkaido. From an early age, he studied Budo (martial arts), and as
he matured, he conducted research into various traditional martial disciplines while also teaching.
Through a fateful encounter, he became a student of Tenshin Shike, and later dedicated himself
fully to the study of Tenshinryu Hyoho.
Thanks to his extensive martial arts background, he was formally appointed as Daihan (assistant
instructor) after only one year of training. Less than four years after entering the style, he
succeeded to the position of 10th Shike of Tenshinryu Hyoho on February 11, 2012 (Heisei 24),
even while still in the midst of his training.
In addition to teaching the style's techniques, he also develops instructional methods, works to
spread the tradition, and manages international outreach to build global recognition. At present,
together with Tenshin Shike and Ide Shike, he is actively engaged in instruction and training to
pass the tradition on to the next generation.
The name Kuwami Masakumo Isshin is a martial name (bugō) bestowed upon him by Tenshin
Shike.
