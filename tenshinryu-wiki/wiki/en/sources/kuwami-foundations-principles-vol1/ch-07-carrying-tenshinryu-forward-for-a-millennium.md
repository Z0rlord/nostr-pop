Carrying Tenshinryu Forward for a Millennium
This is the vision I shared shortly after joining Tenshinryu, with the small group of
students present at the time, under the name of the Tenshinryu Millennium Project.
My thinking was this: if we set our sights on preserving the tradition for just 100
years, we might only last a few decades. But in this rapidly changing era, aiming for a
millennium might allow us to sustain the tradition for at least a century.
