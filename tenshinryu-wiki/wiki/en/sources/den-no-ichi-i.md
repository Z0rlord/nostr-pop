# Source summary — Den no Ichi-i (伝壱位), 2026

**Author:** [[people/kuwami-masakumo|Kuwami Masakumo]], 10th Shike of Tenshinryu Hyoho
**Published:** Shimotsuki (November), Year 8 of the Reiwa Era (2026), Tenshinryu Hyoho. 139 pp., English.

**Scope:** The school's new **required curriculum for beginners**. Tenshinryu's traditional transmission ranks (Den-i 伝位) run seven stages — Hatsu-yurushi, Naka-yurushi, Oku-yurushi, Kirigami, Mokuroku, Menkyo, Kaiden — but under that system even Hatsu-yurushi can take ten years. Den no Ichi-i (伝壱位, "First Transmission Rank") establishes small steps suited to the present day: **ten techniques** regarded as especially important for beginners, in technique and in Shinpō (心法, training of the mind):

- **Zahō (坐法, Seated Techniques) — Okigatana (置刀):** Tsuka-uchi, Tsuba-tengaeshi, Kojiri-gaeshi, Nukiuchi — the Katsujinken (活人剣, "sword that gives life") teachings of Denchū-Tōhō Saya-no-uchi, settling a dispute without drawing, or without cutting.
- **Kodachi-Battōjutsu (小太刀抜刀術):** Nukiuchi no Koto — the basic short-sword draw and body mechanics.
- **Tachiai-Battōjutsu (立相抜刀術):** Kusame-nuki, Tsurube-nuki, Tsurube-otoshi, Ogami-nuki, Tsuri-nuki — standing draws, including the school's gateway training kata.

The opening essays cover Kaisho/Gyōsho/Sōsho and Shu/Ha/Ri, breaking down movements, Jo/Ha/Kyū training speeds, and the three goals set for every technique (name; characteristics — Maai and Situation; sequence, form and movements). Explanations are limited to the Kaisho and Shu stage.
