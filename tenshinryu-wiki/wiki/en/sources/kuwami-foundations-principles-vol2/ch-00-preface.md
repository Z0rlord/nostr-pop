Preface
T his book is an introductory guide for beginners studying T enshinryu. It is a
revised edition of a book originally published in Japan in 2012.
T he foreign-language editions are divided into separate volumes, and this is
the second volume.
While the first volume covered the history and philosophy of T enshinryu,
this volume focuses on fundamental movements, etiquette, and the essential
concepts required for training. T hese teachings are indispensable for anyone
seeking to learn T enshinryu correctly.
D rawing upon the oral teachings, written instructions, and direct
transmission I received from T enshin Sensei, I have explained the
fundamental principles and practices in as much detail as possible.
O f course, this book cannot encompass everything.
T here are aspects of martial arts that cannot be fully expressed in words or
writing. Ultimately, this is a path that each practitioner must come to
embody through many years of dedicated training.
Nevertheless, I am confident that this book will serve as a reliable guide
toward genuine progress.
Build upon the teachings in this book with the knowledge and experience
you gain through daily training. Add your own discoveries and
understanding to its pages, making it a book that grows together with you.
May we continue to learn, improve, and walk this path together for many
years to come.

Year 8 of the Reiwa Era (2026)

Tenshinryu Hyoho, 10th Shike
Kuwami Masakumo
