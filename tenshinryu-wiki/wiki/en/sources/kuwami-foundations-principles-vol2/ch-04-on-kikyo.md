On Kikyo 起居
T his chapter introduces and explains the etiquette of Kikyo 起居 (rising and
sitting movements) within the T enshinryu tradition.
In general usage, Kikyo refers simply to the acts of standing up and sitting
down.
Although these movements are often performed unconsciously in daily life,
they are governed by detailed etiquette in the context of samurai formal
conduct(Buke-Reihō 武家礼法).
T his etiquette is not only about displaying non-hostility and graceful
movement—it also serves to prevent the creation of openings (suki 隙, it
refers to carelessness or an opportunity for the opponent to attack.) as a
warrior, and plays an important role in developing bodily control and
functional awareness.
