Method for Sitting in Koza
T here are multiple methods, but this book presents the most standard one.
At a natural hand position, pinch the hakama and raise it slightly.
For martial-arts hakama, this is generally around the ai-biki domari 相引き止
まり (the bottom end of the side opening), roughly the lower area beneath
that point.
D raw the left foot behind the right to cross the shins, gently bend both
knees, set the left foot on the toes, and place the left knee on the floor.
Next, place the left instep on the floor and lower the hips to sit in Koza 胡
坐.
Especially when wearing tabi 足袋 (Japanese socks), adjust the hakama so
the shins do not show between the hakama and the tabi, and tidy your
appearance.
When in Sagetō 提刀, the hand
holding the sword, while still
holding it, uses the fingers to
pinch up the hakama.
After lowering the hips and sitting
in Koza 胡坐, set the sword down
and straighten the hakama hems.
