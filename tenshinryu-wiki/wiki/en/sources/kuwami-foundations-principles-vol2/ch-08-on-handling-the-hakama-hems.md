On Handling the Hakama Hems
T here are two ways to arrange the hakama hems in ōgi-za 扇坐.
O ne method is to spread them into a triangle, as shown in the photo.
T his creates a neat, visually pleasing shape.
However, when lateral space is tight, the hems can get in the way; and if
swords are placed to either side, the hems may slip under the sword or end
up covering it.
In such cases, use the other method: as shown in the photo, fold the spread
hems into a triangle and tidy them.
D o not leave the hems crumpled; avoid messy handling.
