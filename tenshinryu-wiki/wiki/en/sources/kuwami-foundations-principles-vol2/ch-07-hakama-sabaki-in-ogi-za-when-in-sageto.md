Hakama Sabaki
in Ōgi-za when in Sagetō
Here we explain the hakama handling
used when the sword is carried in the
right hand, that is, in Sagetō 提刀 (下
刀). All movements are performed
one-han ded. Because one hand is
occupied, use this method only when in
Sagetō and never when unarmed. When
both hands are free, always use one of the
four Hakama-Sabaki methods presented
earlier in ‘ Hakama-Sabaki in Ōgiza
(Unarmed)'.

1, Kata-suso Barai 片裾払い
(Single-Hem Sweep)
T his method is intended for beginners;
you sweep one hem at a time.
While bending at the waist, bend the
left wrist inward and, with the left palm,
sweep the left hem as if lightly patting
the inner thigh.
At the same time, place the left knee on

Next, open the right foot slightly to the

front, coming into yagura-za 矢倉坐.
At this point, as with the left knee, set
the right knee with a slight opening to
the right.
T hen place both insteps on the floor
and lower the hips to sit in ōgi-za 扇坐.
Lay the sword to your right, pinch the
ends of the hakama hems with both
hands, lift the hips slightly, and draw
the hems to either side.
Finally, arrange the hems and compose
your posture.

2, Ryo-suso Barai 両裾払い
(Double-Hem Sweep)
T his method sweeps both hems in a
single action. You must sweep quickly.
While bending at the waist, bend the
left wrist inward and, with the left
palm, sweep the left hem as if lightly
patting the inner thigh. Immediately
after, turn the left wrist over and
sweep the right hem just as swiftly.
At the same time, place the left knee
on the floor not straight ahead but
opened slightly to the left. O pen the
right knee so that it faces roughly
sideways.
