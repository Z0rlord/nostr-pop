Points of Caution
— About the right hand
In Tsuka-uchi, after taking up the sword placed at your side, you bring it to the
right hip. In Kojiri-gaeshi, however, take care not to bring the sword to the hip af‐
ter taking it up.
— Strike in a straight line
Do not swing the sword around; strike sharply so that the kojiri traces as straight a
line as possible. The sequence above is explained simply, but if you swing with the
Tsukagashira as a fixed pivot, the kojiri traces a large arc and your counter is de‐
layed accordingly.
Therefore, in practice, the left fist moves to the left.
