Key Learning Points
— Learn correct Shin no Rei
— Learn the posture of opening both knees

Points of Caution
— Do not confuse it with Tsurube-nuki
It resembles Tsurube-nuki, but the method of drawing and other aspects actually
differ, so take care not to confuse them.
— About the path of the right fist when drawing
When drawing, the right fist is not raised straight up; it is raised overhead tracing
an arc from the right.
— About the posture when drawing
Take care not to stretch upward when drawing. Draw by raising only the upper
body without changing the height of the hips.
Also, just before cutting down, keep a slightly forward-leaning posture.
— Extend the elbow when drawing
When swinging the right fist up, extend the right elbow as far as you can.
— About the left hand
After drawing, when taking the tsuka with the left hand, move the left fist and left
elbow in a straight line. In many cases they open outward (to the left) and trace a
curve.
— About the hip joints, knees and standing on tiptoe
Open the hip joints and knees as far as possible, and rise onto the toes to the
extreme.
