However, in today's training environment, according to Ishii Sensei of a former
generation, twenty to thirty years are needed to reach Menkyo.
It is very difficult for busy people today to master Tenshinryu correctly, and there
are too many required techniques; as things stand, even Hatsu-yurushi takes ten
years.
For this reason, we have decided to establish small steps suited to the present day.
This makes the techniques easier to remember, and narrowing down the tech‐
niques makes progress easier. We believe this will help raise motivation for
training.
Another advantage is that textbooks such as this one, and instructional videos, can
be produced easily.

伝壱位

In this Den no Ichi-i (
, "First Transmission Rank"), we have selected ten
techniques: techniques for beginners that are regarded as especially important in
Tenshinryu, and important techniques that contain teachings symbolic of
Tenshinryu.
I hope that you will devote yourself to training, with this as your first step in the
study of Tenshinryu.
Shimotsuki (November), Year 8 of the Reiwa Era (2026)
Tenshinryu Hyoho, 10th Shike
Kuwami Masakumo

天心流初学集

— The photographs and explanations have been prepared as accurately as possible,
but they are still not perfect. Please acquire the details by being taught in person
by the instructors.
— To keep the content as concise as possible, repeated parts have been omitted.

股立

— In the photographs, the momodachi (
) of the hakama is taken (the hem is
tucked up) to show clearly the position of the feet, the bend and direction of the
knees, the opening of the hip joints, etc. Normally, however, the momodachi is
not taken in training.
— The numbers of the movement sequences in the text do not always correspond
to the numbers in the photo sequences.
