One of the characteristics of Tenshinryu is that it transmits a very wide variety of
Zahō Kodachi-Battōjutsu techniques. This is because the shōtō was a genuinely im‐
portant weapon in the actual life of the samurai of the past.

In Den no Ichi-i, only one technique is practiced, as the most basic of the basics. It
is a simple technique, but what you acquire through it is extremely important, and
it is not easy to master, so train in it thoroughly.
