Overview
This is a technique of cutting upward from below.
From its distinctive forward-leaning posture and the form of the upward cut, your
body is likened to an angler, the right arm to a fishing rod, the sword to a fishing
line, and the Taisha's (

Key Learning Points
— Learn Tenjin-barai (Tachi-barai)
— Learn to return the scabbard, which has been turned upside down, during Nōtō
— Learn the beginner's Tenka-chiburui and Gyakute-nōtō (
with a reverse grip)

逆手納刀, sheathing

Points of Caution
— Do not push the fist forward when drawing
Do not let the fist come forward when drawing. Draw as if thrusting the right fist
straight up.
— Keep the right arm against the face
Do not let the right upper arm separate from the right side of the head; raise the
right arm almost perpendicular to the upper body.
— Extend the right arm
Extend the right elbow and take care not to bend it.
— Do not raise the kissaki
Cut and stop the Taisha's draw with the image of catching fingers in a sliding door.
As the name suggests, like a fishing line held at a slant, take care not to flick the
kissaki up, and keep the blade at a slant.
However, do not stop the kissaki deliberately; take care that it comes to a stop
naturally.

— Maintain the Taimen (

When cutting upward, the chest opens slightly to the right, but do not go into
Hanmi (
). Maintain the posture of Taimen.

半身

— About the posture
The feet when cutting upward resemble the feet when cutting down in Ogaminuki, but compared with Ogami-nuki, you do not rise onto the toes as much and do
not open the knees as much.
— About Gyakute-nōtō
When Kissaki Sanzun has been sheathed, the right forearm is roughly vertical.
— About Nōtō
The first part sheathed is said to be Kissaki Sanzun (approx. 9 cm), but this applies
to Tenshinryu's Jōsun (
) of two shaku two sun (66 cm) to just over two
shaku two sun. With a longer sword, correspondingly more than three sun of the
kissaki goes in.

定寸

After sheathing Kissaki Sanzun, continue sheathing while moving the scabbard
slightly faster than the sword.
