About Breaking Down the Movements
Kaisho and Shu refer to the method and stage of training in which the movement
is divided into parts, in order to acquire the technique firmly and to make learning
easier as the first stage of the technique.
For some techniques, a representative way of dividing them has been clearly
handed down, but basically a technique can be divided in countless ways, and the

division can change without limit according to the stage and purpose of the
training.
Since it is impossible to introduce all of them here, only one pattern that can be
called representative is introduced.
If you train firmly in what is described here, you will basically be able to acquire
other divisions easily as well.
