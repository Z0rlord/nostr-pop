# Is Tenshinryu connected to Yagyū Shinkage-ryū?

Yes. Tenshinryu is a branch of Shinkage-ryū, in the Edo Yagyū line.

Our founder, Tokizawa Yahei, studied Shinkage-ryū under Yagyū Sekishūsai and then served Sekishūsai's son, Yagyū Munenori, the sword instructor to the Tokugawa shoguns. Munenori ordered Tokizawa to organize a complete martial system for the Kōgan, the covert corps of samurai that supported the early Tokugawa government. Tokizawa built it from Sekishūsai's teachings, Munenori's own techniques, the methods of Munenori's son Munefuyu, and the Kōgan's tactical doctrine, and named it Tenshinryu in the Kan'ei era (1624 to 1644).

Munenori is, in essence, the true origin of the style. That is why the school's registered name is Edo Yagyū Tenshinryu Hyoho.

Tenshinryu shares many technical elements with Shinkage-ryū, but it is not the same style.
