# Names of the Training Roles

Role terms used in paired practice (Kuwami 2026, "Names of the Training Roles").

| Art | Attacking role | Defending role |
|-----|----------------|----------------|
| Battō (sword drawing) | **kōtōsha 攻刀者** (kōtō) | **bōtōsha 防刀者** (bōtō) |
| Kenjutsu | **uchikata 打方** | **shikata 仕方** |
| Yawara-te 柔手 | **gōte 剛手** | **yawara-te** |

Kō 攻 attack, bō 防 defense, tō 刀 sword, sha 者 the person.

**Note on shikata and yawara-te:** both carry the sense of "the one who performs the technique", the main practitioner. When the technique is itself an attack initiated by that practitioner, the name still applies; the terms mark who does the waza, not strictly who defends.
