# Metsuke 目付 — the Use of the Eyes

Metsuke is how the eyes are directed and attention distributed in training and combat, part of **shinpō 心法**, the mental side of the art. Most information comes through vision (commonly said: about 80 percent), but in combat there is no time to consciously name what you see and decide; practice with known movements is used to weld preparatory sign → movement → correct response into reflex (Kuwami 2026, "Metsuke — The Use of the Eyes").

## Hoshi-Mine-Tani-Yama — fundamental checkpoints

1. **Hoshi 星 (stars):** the fists. One fist is issei 一星, both are nisei 二星. Fist movement is the clearest sign of how the sword is being handled; arguably the most important metsuke.
2. **Mine-tani 峰谷 (peaks and valleys):** the rising and falling of the upper arms (shoulder to elbow). A rising arm ("peak") prepares a downward cut; a lowering arm ("valley") prepares an upward cut.
3. **Yama 山 (mountain), enzan 遠山:** the two shoulders read as one distant mountain; anticipate from their combined movement. Especially important when the fists cannot be seen, as against Suigetsu-no-kurai 水月の位.

## Tachiai-no-shinbō 立相之心法 — perception in an encounter

- **Mi-bure 躰振れ:** subtle signs in the body, especially face and shoulders, preceding action.
- **Ji-bure 地揺れ:** signs in the legs, above all the knees in seated work: short of shin-no-ma 心の間, the opponent must move a knee to reach you at all.
- **Shin-no-me 心の眼:** perceiving the opponent's mind: intention precedes movement, read through words, actions, and indications.
- **Shin-no-me 真の目:** the discerning eye: the precise, detailed reading of movement that the perception above must be built on.
- **Kasumi-me 霞目:** the mist-like gaze; eyes slightly narrowed, never fixed or stuck.
- **Kan-no-me 観の眼:** observing the opponent's whole form, information from the whole rather than one point.
- **Tōgan 透眼 / sukashi-me 透目:** seeing through: behind the opponent's tsuba, into how their [[concepts/tenouchi|tenouchi]] is working.
- **Yō-no-me 陽の目 and kage-no-me 影の目:** seeing the visible and the concealed (hidden weapons, schemes, feints), together yōei-no-koto 陽影の事. Ishii Sensei's oral teaching: 「極意は睫の如くに身近にあり影の目を養え」, "The deepest teachings are as close to you as your eyelashes. Cultivate kage-no-me."
- **Sute-me 捨て目 / nagashi-me 流眄:** unobtrusive observation of the surroundings, checking behind you without showing it, eyes moving without the head: for footing, concealed opponents, allies, escape routes.
