**Yakujō (Kinchō) — the Oath that Opens and Closes Training** — Resumo provisório: Yakujō means a promise or agreement. In Tenshinryu it names the ceremonial kinchō performed at the beginning and end of every training session (Kuwami 2026, "On Yakujō").
