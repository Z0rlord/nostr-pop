**Kesa 袈裟** — Resumo provisório: “Kesagake(袈裟懸け)” means to hang something diagonally from the shoulder. In Tenshin-ryu, it refers to cutting diagonally. This term originates from the monk’s garment, the kesa(袈裟). Emperor Go-Mizunoo
